What is SAENRU?

SAENRU is a Korean AI builder launch platform founded in 2023 in Seoul. Builders publish AI-made websites and web apps for free, share their GitHub repositories through open launches, and collect votes and feedback from the community. For teams without time to build, our studio ships production sites and MVPs; for brands, our AEO·GEO consulting makes them visible inside ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity answers. This very website is built with our own pipeline — including the structured data an AI crawler is reading right now.
