Scan Protocol

What AI looks at
before it cites anyone

Answer engines check three gates first. Fail any one of them and your content quality doesn't matter — you're out of the citation pool.

G1

Crawler access

GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and five other AI crawlers must not be blocked in robots.txt. If this gate is closed, nothing else matters.

G2

Raw HTML content

AI crawlers don't wait for JavaScript. Your actual content must exist in the first HTML response the server sends.

G3

Schema in raw HTML

JSON-LD structured data must live in the raw HTML. Schema injected later by tag managers is often invisible to crawlers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly are AEO and GEO?
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes your brand citable by AI answer engines like ChatGPT; GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) does the same for generative AI search. Where SEO fought for rankings, AEO·GEO aims to put you inside the single answer an AI gives.
Can you guarantee AI mentions?
Be suspicious of anyone who guarantees it. AI answers vary by model and moment, so this is about raising probability, not certainty. We measure before and after with live data and report both honestly.
Do you work with non-Korean brands?
Yes. We work in Korean and English, and the measurement methodology is language-independent. For English-market engagements we run English question sets against the AI engines.
What does the live console on this page measure?
It's a self-demonstration: your browser fetches this page's robots.txt, raw HTML, and JSON-LD exactly the way an AI crawler would (gates G1–G3). External domains can't be measured in-browser due to CORS, which is what the official diagnostic is for.
